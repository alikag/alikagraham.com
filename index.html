I'm an investor and operator building a portfolio of service businesses under IMPAKT. I like messy problems, durable businesses, and small teams doing real work.

Today that means Pink's Windows across the Hudson Valley and Palm Beach, East Bench Overlook, a residential land development in Montana, and Instant SDR, an AI-powered sales development platform. Each one has its own manager on the ground. My job is capital, systems, and the long view.

My background is data and analytics. I was a partner at impakt Advisors, which we sold to Croud in 2021. That shapes how I run things now: I build the internal tooling, CRM automations, and AI infrastructure that let operator-led businesses punch above their weight.
